🧠📝 Memory Verse

"For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again." - Matthew 7:2 ⚖️📏🔁🪞⚠️

Text: Genesis 14:18-20

Introduction 🧠✨📖

A greater number of the world's population is a victim of conviction through the examples of the past and only a few people dare the unknown. Almost everyone wants to know if others have done it, seen it or aggrees with it and until that is ascertained they won't be involved. This lesson will show some examples of tithe payers in the Bible which may enable us to comply with this divine demand.

Aims and Objectives 🎯📖💡

Aim: To clear the doubts in the minds of God's children as to whether the step to pay tithes is reasonable. 💭🕊️📖💰🤔

Objective: Our desire is to see the Christian believers faithfully paying tithes of all unto God . 🙏💰🌱

Text Review/Lesson 📚✨

Text: Genesis 14:18-20 🕊️

Lesson 🧠💡

We have quite a number of withesses in the Bible who gave their tithes to God on various occassions. The text also reveals one of such examples whom God blessed before and after tithing. Let us consider few examples from the Bible.

  1. Abraham - Genesis 14:20

  2. Jacob - Genesis 28:22

  3. Levi who paid through Abraham - Hebrews 7:9

  4. Hezekiah and Israel - 2 Chronicles 31:4-7

  5. Nehemiah and Israel - Nehemiah 13:9-13

  6. Pharisees - Matthew 23:23

  7. Early Christians - 1 Corinthians 9:9-14, 1 Timothy 5:17-18, Galatians 6:6

    Prayer Points

    Thank You, Lord, for the examples of faithful tithe payers in the Bible. May their stories inspire us to honor You with our tithes.
    Lord, let my mindset be transformed to understand the importance of tithing as a biblical principle, not just an obligation.
    Lord, I pray for grace to faithfully obey Your command to tithe, that I may walk in Your blessings rather than face Your discipline.
    Father, following Jacob's example of commitment, give me the grace to faithfully honor You with a tenth of all You bless me with.
    Holy Spirit, like Hezekiah who restored proper tithing practices, help me maintain consistency and integrity in my tithing commitment.
    Lord, inspire me through the examples of the early Christians who supported Your work, to give generously and willingly for the advancement of Your kingdom.
    Father, help me follow these biblical examples of tithing with the right heart attitude, focusing not just on the act but also on justice, mercy and faithfulness.
    Lord, like Nehemiah who restored proper tithing after the exile, help me maintain faithful tithing practices and inspire others through my example.
